Privacy Policy

WC Sweepstakes

Last updated: 10 June 2026

Summary

WC Sweepstakes does not require an account, does not use analytics or advertising, and does not share data with third parties for marketing. The only data the app handles is what's needed to run a shared sweepstake: the player names entered into a sweepstake and an anonymous device identifier.

What data is collected

  • Player names. When a sweepstake is created, the organiser enters a name for each participant (for example a first name or nickname). These names are stored on our server so that everyone in the sweepstake sees the same draw and standings.
  • Anonymous device identifier. The app generates a random identifier on your device (a UUID). It is sent to our server when you claim your spot in a sweepstake, so the app knows which entry belongs to your device. It is not derived from your hardware, Apple ID, or any personal information, and it cannot be used to identify you outside the app.
  • Sweepstake data. The sweepstake itself — its name, team assignments, and settings — is stored on our server so all participants can access it via the room code.

What is not collected

  • No accounts, email addresses, or phone numbers.
  • No location data.
  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising identifiers.
  • No contacts, photos, or other device data.

Where data is stored

Sweepstake data is stored on a server operated by us using Cloudflare's infrastructure. Match results shown in the app are fetched from the same service and contain no personal data. Claim and admin tokens are stored only on your device, in the iOS Keychain.

How long data is kept

Sweepstake data is kept for the duration of the tournament so participants can view results, and is deleted after the sweepstake is no longer active. You can ask for a sweepstake or a player name to be removed at any time by contacting us.

Your rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal data (such as a player name) by emailing the address below. Since the app holds no account information, please include the sweepstake room code so we can locate the data.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Player names are entered by the sweepstake organiser, and we encourage organisers to use first names or nicknames only.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
